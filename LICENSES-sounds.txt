STICK WAR LIVE - sound effects
==============================
All sound files in this folder were cut, trimmed, normalised and converted to OGG from the
packs below. Every pack used is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
free for commercial use, no attribution required. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
(Credits are listed anyway, as thanks.) Downloaded from OpenGameArt.org on 29 Oct 2026.
